## External Review

`werner.btc` of Leather Support provided written technical feedback on wallet-signing
assumptions, sponsor-fee approval binding, backup verification, malformed-announcement
resilience, key custody disclosures, and privacy claims. The findings were addressed and
regression-tested. This is not a formal audit, Leather endorsement, or mainnet signoff.

## Independent Reproduction

The second non-team pair has completed the full publicly visible transaction sequence:

- Wallet F registered P/V on mainnet.
- Wallet E funded and settled two private payments.
- The two one-time addresses received 1,000 and 980 sats.
- Both one-time keys authorized sponsored full withdrawals, leaving both origins at zero sBTC.

This proves onchain registration, funding, settlement, possession of the derived
one-time spend keys, and withdrawal. The same non-team tester confirmed in writing that
they restored the backup in a fresh browser/session, scanned locally, followed the public
guide themselves, and completed the withdrawal. Privara retains the confirmation
privately and does not publish identifying or recovery information.
